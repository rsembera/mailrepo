If your password has been compromised and you believe others may have access to your backups, changing the password is not enough on its own. Your mail is encrypted under a master key that a password change does not replace, and every backup contains a copy of it — so someone who can open one old backup with the old password can open every later one too. Rotating the master key closes that: your whole archive is re-encrypted under a new key, and no earlier password, recovery key or backup can open anything current.

You will be given a new recovery key at the end; the current one stops working. This may take several minutes or longer depending on the size of your archive. Your mail is not modified.
