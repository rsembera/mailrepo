Changing your password or recovery key replaces the wrapper around the master key; the master key itself stays the same. That is enough when a credential leaked but no one has a copy of your archive.

Rotate the master key when you think someone has both — an old backup and the password or recovery key that opened it. Rotation re-encrypts every message and stored credential under a new master key, rekeys the database, and issues a new recovery key. Afterwards, no earlier password, recovery key, or backup opens anything current. Earlier backups become snapshots only.

Takes seconds for a typical archive, a couple of minutes for a very large one. Your mail is not modified.
