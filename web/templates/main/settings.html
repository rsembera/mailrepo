App-Specific Passwords

If you use two-factor authentication (2FA) with your email provider, you'll need to generate an app-specific password for MailRepo.

An app password is a one-time password that lets apps access your account without needing your main password or 2FA code.

How to get one:

  • Gmail: Go to your Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords
  • iCloud: Go to appleid.apple.com → Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords
  • Outlook/Hotmail: Go to account.microsoft.com → Security → Advanced security options → App passwords
  • Other providers: Look for "App passwords" or "Third-party app access" in your account security settings

If your email provider doesn't require 2FA, you can use your regular password.
